# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Project participants should:

- discuss technical work respectfully and in good faith;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- keep reviews focused on the work; and
- protect credentials, private infrastructure details and personal data.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity and to project discussions in other
public or private channels when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@hybridops.tech`. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and as promptly as practical. Depending on severity,
the response may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction or
permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Concerns involving the lead maintainer may also be reported through GitHub's
platform reporting process.
